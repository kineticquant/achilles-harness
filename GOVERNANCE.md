# Achilles governance

This repository is a **fork of [goose](https://github.com/aaif-goose/goose)** used to build **Achilles** (the harness) and **Arrav** (the optional local model). Goose and AAIF do not endorse Achilles.

## Where work happens

- Issues and pull requests: [kineticquant/achilles-harness](https://github.com/kineticquant/achilles-harness)
- Product docs: [`docs/`](docs/README.md)
- License: Apache 2.0 — see [LICENSING.md](LICENSING.md), [LICENSE-APACHE](LICENSE-APACHE), and [NOTICE](NOTICE)

Upstream goose has its own maintainers, Discord, and [governance](https://github.com/aaif-goose/goose/blob/main/GOVERNANCE.md). That process does **not** apply here. Do not file Achilles bugs on the goose Issues board.

## Contributions

Open an issue or PR on this repo. Keep goose-derived Apache notices intact. Do not mass-rename internal `goose` crate or CLI identifiers unless that is an explicit, scoped change.

Tie-breakers and merge rights sit with the people who operate `origin` on this fork.
